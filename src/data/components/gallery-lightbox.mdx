A lightbox sits at the intersection of three concerns that rarely live in the
same library: an accessible modal shell, a draggable carousel, and a per-slide
zoom gesture. Reaching for one batteries-included widget collapses all three
into a single opinion. Reaching for three primitives keeps each concern in the
library that handles it best — Radix Dialog for focus trap and Escape, Embla for
drag/snap/loop, Motion for the zoom-and-pan inside a slide.

The trick that makes the composition work is the handoff. Embla wants to claim
the horizontal drag, and Motion wants to pan a zoomed slide along the same axis.
Embla's `watchDrag` option takes a predicate, so a shared `isZoomedRef` lets
Embla yield the gesture the moment the active slide crosses past `scale: 1`.
Tap to zoom, drag to pan; release the zoom, and Embla picks the drag back up.
